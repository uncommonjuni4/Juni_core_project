Policies & Safeguarding

Clear policies help protect students, guide staff and build trust with families.

Safeguarding is not one document. It is a set of responsibilities, reporting routes, safety procedures and everyday practices designed to keep students supported and protected.

Safeguarding Framework View Policy Areas

Safety Principles

Prevent

Clear expectations, safe routines and trained staff reduce avoidable risks.

Report

Students, families and staff should know how to raise a concern safely.

Respond

Concerns should reach the right authorised person and be handled promptly.

Policy titles and dates are demo placeholders until official school documents are connected.

Safeguarding Framework

Student safety is a shared responsibility with clear ownership.

Teachers, school administration, leadership and families all contribute to safeguarding. The key is knowing who must act, what must be recorded and when a concern needs escalation.

Recognise Concerns

Staff remain alert to changes in behaviour, attendance, wellbeing and student safety.

Report Properly

Concerns should be shared through approved channels, not informal discussion.

Protect Privacy

Sensitive student information is shared only with authorised people who need it.

Escalate When Needed

Urgent or serious concerns move quickly to school leadership and appropriate external channels where required.

Safeguarding Promise

Listen carefully. Record responsibly. Act appropriately.

Behaviour & Anti-Bullying

Respectful behaviour is part of student safety.

Behaviour policies should be clear enough that students understand expectations, adults respond consistently and families know how concerns will be handled.

Clear Expectations

Consistent Response

Support & Follow-Up

School Culture

Concerns, Complaints & Reporting

Families and students should know how to raise a concern and what happens next.

Different issues belong to different routes. Clear reporting reduces confusion and helps the school respond more effectively.

Academic / Class Concern

Start with the assigned teacher where appropriate, then escalate if the issue remains unresolved.

Official Record / Service Issue

Use the relevant request workflow for profile, fee, document, transport or official correction concerns.

Safety / Safeguarding Concern

Safety concerns should move directly to the authorised safeguarding or leadership route rather than waiting through ordinary service channels.
