Our Beginning

The school began with a simple focus: clear teaching, strong routines and close support for students.

JuniCore School started as a smaller learning community with a focus on accessible education, dependable classroom practice and close communication between teachers and families.

As student numbers and academic needs grew, the school expanded carefully—adding new learning stages, specialist teaching, improved facilities and stronger systems for student support.

Founding Principle

Grow carefully without losing the personal support students need.

2011

Foundation

School established with early primary classes.

The first phase focused on building strong classroom routines, family trust and a stable learning environment.

2014

Expansion

Primary section expanded and new classrooms added.

Growing enrolment led to improved classroom capacity and a more structured learning-stage model.

2017

Middle School

Middle School introduced with specialist subject teaching.

Students gained access to deeper subject learning, project work, science activities and stronger study routines.

2020

Digital Shift

Digital learning and communication systems expanded.

The school increased its use of digital tools for learning resources, notices, parent communication and academic coordination.

2022

Senior School

Senior School pathway strengthened for Grade 9–10.

Exam preparation, mentoring, academic tracking and senior subject support became more structured.

2025

Campus Growth

Facilities and student support services expanded.

Classrooms, labs, reading areas, transport coordination and wellbeing support were strengthened across the campus.

2026

JuniCore Era

JuniCore digital school ecosystem introduced.

School website, Admin, Teacher, Student and Parent experiences are brought into one connected system for better visibility and communication.
